# Description

Developing a physics-guided machine learning algorithm to estimate the land surface temperature from satellite imagery for early detection of unsafe temperatures in collaboration with [DPhi Space](https://www.dphispace.com/).
We are combining physical models and neural networks to use the high-resolution [Sentinel-2](https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/copernicus/sentinel-2) satellite imagery over Europe to recover a super-resolution version of the land surface temperature recorded by [Landsat](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/landsat/).
