# Description

Led several master and bachelor student projects in the field of machine learning, focusing on foundation models, diffusion models, physicalising latent spaces, and physics-informed neural networks.
The aim of these projects was to provide students with hands-on experience in applying machine learning techniques to real-world problems, while also contributing to our research in these areas.
The projects are:
- Foundation Models - Training and fine-tuning [AstroPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14930) on simulations of galaxy clusters to construct informative latent spaces for dark matter model classification.
- Diffusion Models - Improve the quality weak gravitational lensing observations of galaxy clusters by reducing noise and filling in missing information.
- Physicalising Latent Spaces - Train a neural network to predict the dark matter model posterior distribution from the latent space of deep neural networks trained on simulations of galaxy clusters.
- Physics-Informed Neural Network - Accelerate hydrodynamic simulations by training a neural network to predict the evolution of molecular clouds over time, using physical equations.

Alongside the projects, I also taught first-year problem classes at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/) on mechanics 101 and thermodynamics.
