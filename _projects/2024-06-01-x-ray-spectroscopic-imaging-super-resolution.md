# Description

Prototyping a super-resolution point cloud autoencoder for the X-ray spectroscopic imaging telescopes [Chandra](https://chandra.harvard.edu/) and the proposed [Line Emission Mapper](https://www.nasa.gov/press-release/nasa-selects-line-emission-mapper-mission-to-study-cosmic-web) (LEM).
The autoencoder is trained on simulated data to learn to combine information from point clouds of low-spectral high-spatial resolution and high-spectral low-spatial, enabling the reconstruction of high-resolution images from low-resolution observations.
The project aims to improve the spatial resolution of X-ray telescopes, allowing for more detailed studies of astrophysical phenomena.
